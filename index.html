Privacy Policy for ShopNAll

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

At ShopNAll ("we," "our," or "us"), accessible from our application and website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and recorded by ShopNAll and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with seamless local shop discovery, live messaging, and route navigation, we collect the following types of information:

  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect precise or approximate real-time geographic location data from your device to show nearby shops and provide turn-by-turn map navigation.
  • Communications & Messages: Content from messages sent through our in-app chat drawer (between you and local shop owners or assistants) to facilitate your inquiries.
  • Usage & Device Information: Standard log data including IP addresses, browser types, device information, and interactions with shop listings to improve user experience.
  • Account Information: (If applicable) Name, email address, or contact details provided during sign-up or profile creation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Display nearby shops, products, and localized deals on the map.
  • Enable navigation and route planning to physical store locations.
  • Facilitate real-time communications between users and local merchants.
  • Maintain, improve, and personalize the app experience.
  • Ensure platform safety, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

3. Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following scenarios:

  • With Merchants/Shops: Messages you send via the chat feature are shared directly with the specific shop owner or customer support representative you are communicating with.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: We may use third-party APIs (e.g., map tile and routing services like Google Maps, Leaflet, or Mapbox) to display maps and compute directions. These providers process location queries in accordance with their privacy policies.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental regulations.

4. Location Controls & Data Retention

  • Location Permissions: You can enable or disable location services at any time through your device or browser settings. Disabling location services will restrict real-time navigation and distance-based shop discovery.
  • Data Retention: Chat logs and profile information are stored securely only for as long as necessary to fulfill the services outlined in this policy.

5. Third-Party Links & Services

ShopNAll may contain links to external shop websites or third-party mapping services. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

6. Children's Information

ShopNAll does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this kind of information on our platform, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
